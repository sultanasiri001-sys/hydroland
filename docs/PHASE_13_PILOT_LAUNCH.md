# Phase 13 — Pilot launch playbook

Status: ready for controlled pilot preparation.

## Entry gates
- Merge validated phases in order and deploy only to the selected sandbox/production environment.
- Appoint one licensed pilot dive centre and one accountable operations lead.
- Verify current SWSDF, SRSA, Zawil and TGA requirements with the relevant authority.
- Approve RTO/RPO, restore test evidence, incident contacts, privacy/retention policy and support escalation.

## Pilot scope
- Limited invited users only; no public marketing claim.
- One region, selected shore/boat activity and capped capacity.
- Manual human approval for activation, safety, refunds, contracts and regulated exceptions.
- Daily review of bookings, safety decisions, support tickets, audit events and failed webhooks.

## Success criteria
- No unresolved critical security or safety event.
- Capacity protection, role controls and payment idempotency remain valid.
- Pilot users complete onboarding, booking and post-trip feedback.
- Operations owner signs the pilot exit report.

## Exit decision
A human release committee decides GO, REVIEW_REQUIRED or NO_GO. An AI agent cannot approve launch or override safety/regulatory controls.
