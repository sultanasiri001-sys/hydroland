# Human employee onboarding from the control center

The employee administration screen creates a pending Account/Person through existing verification, or links a verified active account without duplicating identity. An explicit, idempotent setup action anchors the internal HYDROLAND organization and canonical HQ/14-department OrgUnit hierarchy. Position, Employment, HrCandidate and EmploymentMovement remain authoritative; no center-owned or legacy WorkforceSeat record is copied, altered or migrated.

An appointment starts at HR_REVIEW with a PENDING_APPROVAL employment. Under the responsibility rule approved on 23 October 2026, the canonical internal organization's owner reviews its employees, including requests the owner created. The responsible owner must still have an active, verified ADMIN account. Another administrator cannot assume this review merely by holding ADMIN. The employee cannot review or approve their own appointment. Final appointment approval belongs to that owner or an explicitly delegated active internal employee, even when the owner requested and reviewed the appointment. It reuses the existing fail-closed HR compliance checks within the same transaction. No HR role is granted to administrators by the workflow-capacity adapter.

Responsibility follows the canonical organization owner account, not a hardcoded email address or a client-selected reviewer. Ownership and participant authority are checked inside the write transaction. A changed owner must re-review a pending appointment before approval; a prior review cannot authorize the new owner automatically. Review audit events retain the previous reviewer and explicitly record when requester and responsible reviewer are the same account. IAM verifies the same owner-review rule and current owner/delegate approval authority before granting any role.

Center staff remain in the center's own invitation and membership-management workflow. An active center OWNER or authorized ADMIN with the active DIVE_CENTER role manages that center's ordinary members. Another center and the internal platform-owner account have no membership-management authority through these routes without the center's own authorized membership. Accepting invitations, ordinary-role restrictions, self/owner protection and platform-account status remain separate. This change does not create center HR appointments through the internal employee API.

Permission proposals and retry receipts use versioned OperationalSetting metadata keyed to canonical movement IDs; they do not duplicate identity, employment, assignment or workflow status. Serializable writes update a shared synchronization row so waiting transactions retry with a current snapshot. Reusing a request key with the same normalized payload returns the same movement; changing its payload conflicts. A cancelled/rejected pending employment can be requested again without duplicating its employee record, retaining the earlier movement.

IAM verifies the approved appointment and its account, organization, unit, position, candidate reviewer and permission proposal before granting access. Profiles are ordinary STAFF, scoped HR_REVIEWER and scoped HR_MANAGER; HR profiles are limited to the HR department. ADMIN, executive approval and IAM_SERVICE are never selectable grants. Existing active scope is preserved/extended only as approved; suspended/archived role assignments and inactive memberships require their own review and are not silently reactivated. Internal membership is separate from external-center membership. Account suspension remains independent of employment.

Appointment activation, scoped roles, internal membership, session revocation and audit are atomic. The recipient needs a new login after grants. Email provisioning uses the existing challenge/outbox and trial-recipient restriction; Google provisioning sends no email and stays pending until the existing verified-Google flow proves the matching email. Google availability does not verify the new account itself. Random initial password hashes and authentication challenges are never returned to administrators. Email verification permits the owner to set a password through existing password recovery; no temporary password is shared.

The UI provides explicit initialization, account lookup, create/link forms, job/department/profile selection, pending/current permission labels, review/approval/rejection/cancellation, search and cursor pages. It captures fields before asynchronous authorization, fences all late responses by view/session, preserves notes on failed/conflicting writes and clears private data on navigation, role loss and logout.

Validation uses isolated HTTP/PostgreSQL and a loopback email provider, including actual scoped HR endpoint access after activation. It covers one-time verification, duplicate/retry/concurrent writes, responsible-owner review of its own request, owner final approval, unassigned administrator denial, scoped employee delegation/revocation, current-owner revocation and transfer re-review, compliance denial, audit/IAM rollback, fresh-login grants and retained center membership/equipment. Actual center-owner membership changes are tested alongside cross-center and internal/center denial. The same administratively created employee also completes suspension, denied old-session/new-login access, reactivation and successful fresh login, with old tokens still denied and employment/roles retained. Browser regressions cover the connected UI, responsible-review explanation and privacy/navigation boundaries. No migration or identity/role grant is performed by publishing this change. Initialization, review and real onboarding remain explicit administrative actions.

The employee list also reports a read-only, uncached approval-prerequisite snapshot. It counts active, verified ADMIN accounts, reports whether the responsible owner is available and whether the current actor is that reviewer, and uses the same production HR-compliance check as appointment approval. One active verified ADMIN owner is sufficient for appointment review and approval. Delegates need no ADMIN assignment; their current internal employment, STAFF assignment, membership, verification and the owner’s authority are checked on every approval. Missing owner authority and missing/invalid controls are explicit, without exposing owner identities, control references or evidence. Draft requests and responsible review remain available before final approval prerequisites are complete. The diagnostic grants no role, changes no policy and sends no message. Invalid, missing or failed observations display as unknown; refresh clears old observations, preserves draft fields and cannot restore private data after navigation or loss of access.

This completes the human onboarding implementation portion of #472 alongside account suspension/reactivation from #488. Live production acceptance with a dedicated employee remains distinct from isolated test evidence. Managed AI execution and explicit connection diagnostics are documented in `AI_AGENT_RUNTIME_2026-10-22.md`; actual paid generation remains untested. #472 stays open until the dedicated acceptance conditions are met.

Reference preparation for the HR compliance gate uses the independent control-review workflow in `HR_CONTROL_REFERENCE_REVIEW_2026-10-23.md`. Saving a proposed reference does not activate it or alter an existing effective control.

The owner manages approval delegates by exact employee email under `admin/employees/approval-delegations`, with a reason, confirmation and optimistic revision. Only an active internal employee with a verified active account, active STAFF assignment, active membership and active department/position is eligible. Delegates may approve appointments across all internal departments through `employee-approvals`, reached from their account menu; they cannot manage delegates, request employees through ADMIN routes, review candidates as the owner, access unrelated administration or approve their own appointment. Revocation takes effect for subsequent requests using existing sessions. Account suspension, STAFF or membership revocation, employment suspension and any canonical organization revision (including ownership transfer and transfer back) invalidate eligibility; explicit new grants are required after organization changes. Grants are account-bound rather than email-bound, capped at 20, audited atomically, and never create a RoleAssignment.

This owner/delegate policy applies only to internal appointment approval. It does not relax independent legal control-reference approval, compensation, discipline, termination, IAM offboarding or center membership authorization. Legal control references must still be genuine and approved before appointment activation. No production delegate is created until the owner selects an eligible employee.
