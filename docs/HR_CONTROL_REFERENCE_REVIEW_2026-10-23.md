# HR control references and independent review

The employee workspace includes an explicit expandable reference-review panel. It saves a proposed control's requirement identifier, version, evidence identifiers, Legal Governance approval reference and reason. These are references to the authoritative registry and evidence owned by Legal Governance, not a second regulatory library or a machine-generated legal opinion. No statutory requirements, reference values or production controls are seeded by deployment.

`GET /admin/employees/compliance-controls` is uncached and requires a freshly active, verified platform ADMIN. POST creates an immutable, idempotent proposal in versioned OperationalSetting request metadata. A different administrator reviews it; a third administrator, independent of both, explicitly approves and activates it. Every participant must still be active and verified at approval. The server validates bounded fields and rejects client attempts to supply validation status, optionality or workflow identities. The final reviewer acknowledgement includes the Legal Governance reference and effect on HR approvals. Administrators record the review outcome; the software does not establish the legal authority or factual correctness of an external reference on their behalf.

PolicyControl remains the sole effective control record. A draft, review or rejection never changes the effective control. Approval compares the policy revision captured on submission, then atomically stores the references, mandatory/applicable flags, validation result and review provenance, marks the request applied and records an audit event. The event retains the previous effective control metadata and the new references. Concurrent approvals cannot apply twice, and another proposal against an older policy revision must be resubmitted. Failed audits roll back all changes.

The general policy upsert and state-change APIs reject HR_COMPLIANCE writes so they cannot bypass this workflow. They continue serving other policy categories. Existing HR control records are not silently rewritten or adopted; changes require an explicit proposal against their current revision. Existing production HR compliance enforcement still validates effective controls at each sensitive action.

The browser loads private control data only when its panel is expanded. Draft input survives failed writes and refresh, retries keep the request ID, and a changed effective revision clears acknowledgement before resubmission. Decisions capture the reviewed fields before asynchronous authorization. Navigation, logout and revoked access clear private references and fence late responses. Provider traffic and agent tasks are independent of this workflow.

Verification covers actual HTTP/PostgreSQL guard denial, bounded input, missing evidence, repeat and concurrent requests, maker/reviewer/approver separation, participant revocation, audit rollback, stale policy revisions, previous metadata retention and the generic policy bypass boundary. Browser coverage includes draft retry, independent review/approval, conflict notes, changed-revision acknowledgement, access loss, late responses, escaped references, pagination and mobile layout. Test references are isolated fixtures, not approved legal requirements.

Production acceptance remains open in #472. It requires identified independent participants, a dedicated employee test identity, actual approved reference/evidence data and a documented human acceptance session. Deploying the workflow does not populate references, approve a control, grant roles or perform paid model generation.
