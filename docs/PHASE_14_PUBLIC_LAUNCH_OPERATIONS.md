# Phase 14 — Public launch and continuous operations

## Launch gates
A human release committee confirms: merged validated changes; approved pilot exit report; current regulatory approvals; production host/domain; KMS-managed secrets; managed PostgreSQL with PITR; private versioned document storage; restore drill; monitoring and incident escalation; selected payment and communication providers; published privacy, terms and support contacts.

## Operating model
- 24/7 health monitoring and security alerts.
- Daily operational and audit review during launch period.
- Incident triage: safety/regulatory events take priority and suspend affected operations.
- Weekly security patch and dependency review.
- Monthly restore test, access review, cost review and KPI report.
- Human approval for money movement, contracts, production permissions and safety/regulatory exceptions.

## KPIs
Availability, booking completion, payment success, support response time, safety decision outcomes, incident time-to-contain, restoration time, active centres and customer satisfaction.

## Launch decision
Until every launch gate has documented evidence, status remains REVIEW_REQUIRED. AI agents support reporting only and cannot change the decision.
